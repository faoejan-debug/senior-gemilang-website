About the company

Supporting industry through dependable procurement.

CV. Senior Gemilang is an Industrial Supply & Procurement Solutions company established on 8 October 2018. We provide and source industrial products, mechanical spare parts, maintenance components and operational supplies based on customer requirements.

Sourcing can be based on part numbers, drawings, samples, materials, dimensions, technical specifications, OEM references or application requirements. We aim to be a responsive, flexible and competitive procurement partner for routine and special requirements.
